q1)
  quality assurance -  focuses on improving the processes used to develop software and preventing defects
  quality control - focuses on identifying defects in the software product by inspecting and testing 

q2)
  testing shows the presence of defects - testing can show that defects exist in the software 
  exhaustive testing is impossible - it is not possible to test all possible inputs conditions and scenarios in software applications
  early testing saves time and money - testing should begin as early as possible in the software development life cycle
  defect cluster together - a small number of modules usually contain most of the defects discovered during testing
  pesticide paradox - repeating the same test cases again and again may eventually stop finding new defects
