1 levels of testing:-
* unit testing checks individual components to identify defects
* integration testing verifies communication between different modules
* system testing evaluates the complete application to ensure all components work correctly
* acceptance testing confirms whether the software meets user requirements and business expectations before final release

2 five principles of testing:-
* testing shows the presence of defects but cannot prove their absence
* exhaustive testing is impossible because all combinations cannot be tested
* early testing helps identify defects and reduces costs
* defects usually cluster in specific modules
* testing depends on the application context and requires suitable approaches

3 agile methodology:-
agile methodology is an iterative and incremental software development approach 
where projects are divided into small cycles called sprints teams collaborate 
regularly with customers and developers continuously test and improve the software 
feedback helps identify issues early and supports changing requirements agile delivers 
working software frequently improves quality reduces risks and increases customer satisfaction 
through continuous communication and collaboration
